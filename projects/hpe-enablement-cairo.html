OVERVIEW

The HPE event ran from 10 to 15 May in Cairo. On 11 May, two technical and sales enablement sessions at the JW Marriott Club House brought partners together for presentations and networking.

SESSION ATTENDANCE

The post-event report recorded 54 attendees in the morning session and 15 in the afternoon, for a reported total of 69.

EVENT HIGHLIGHTS

  • Guest registration and round-table meeting setup
  • Three coffee breaks and a lunch for attendees to network
  • HPE-branded backpacks and carrying cases
